# **Standard Operating Procedure: First Notice of Loss (FNOL) \- Auto Insurance**

**Document ID:** SOP-FNOL-NS-001  
**Last Updated:** March 1, 2026  
**Department:** Claims Customer Service  
**Applies To:** Tier 1 Auto Claims Agents  
**System Role:** National Sentinel Insurance Source of Truth for Call Workflows

## **1\. Purpose and Scope**

This Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) serves as the primary source of truth for handling inbound First Notice of Loss (FNOL) calls at National Sentinel Insurance. Agents and AI intake assistants must strictly adhere to the workflows outlined here. The goal is to capture incident details, ensure customer safety, establish empathy, and trigger the downstream claims adjudication process within the ClaimSphere system.

## **2\. Initial Call Handling and Empathy**

When a National Sentinel policyholder calls to report an accident, agents must prioritize safety and empathy over data collection.

### **2.1 Mandatory Call Opening (Compliance Script)**

Agents must use the exact structure below for opening the call. *Deviation from this script is a Level 1 compliance violation.*

* **Greeting:** "Thank you for calling National Sentinel Insurance Claims. My name is \[Agent Name\]. This call is recorded for quality and compliance purposes."  
* **Safety Check:** "Before we begin, are you currently in a safe location, and is anyone in need of emergency medical assistance?"  
  * *If NO to safety/medical:* Advise the caller to hang up and dial 911 immediately.  
  * *If YES to safety:* Proceed to the empathy statement.  
* **Empathy Statement:** "I am so sorry to hear that you've been involved in an incident. I know this can be a stressful time, but I am here to help you get this resolved."

## **3\. Policyholder Verification (PII/HIPAA)**

Before discussing policy limits, the caller must be verified to prevent unauthorized disclosure of Personally Identifiable Information (PII).

* Ask for the **Policy Number** (starts with NS-) or the **Primary Phone Number**.  
* Verify two (2) of the following data points against the ClaimSphere dashboard:  
  1. First and Last Name of the Named Insured  
  2. Date of Birth (DOB) of the Named Insured  
  3. The complete 17-character Vehicle Identification Number (VIN)  
  4. The physical mailing address on file

## **4\. Data Gathering: The FNOL Interview**

Once verified, guide the customer through the FNOL interview using conversational prompts. Document all findings directly into the ClaimSphere Intake Form.

### **4.1 Incident Details**

* **Date and Time of Loss:** Exact timestamp of the incident.  
* **Location of Loss:** Street address, intersection, or highway mile marker.  
* **Weather/Road Conditions:** Raining, snowing, dark, or icy.  
* **Description of Loss:** Let the customer explain in their own words. Document facts only. Do not assign fault.

### **4.2 Vehicle and Damage Information**

* Confirm which vehicle on the National Sentinel policy was involved.  
* Ask about the point of impact (e.g., front bumper, driver-side door, rear quarter panel).  
* Determine drivability: "Is the vehicle currently drivable, or does it need to be towed?"

### **4.3 Third-Party Information**

* Third-party driver's name, contact info, and insurance carrier.  
* Year, make, model, and license plate of the third-party vehicle.

### **4.4 Authorities and Police Reports**

* Were the police called? If yes, which department?  
* Obtain the police report number or incident number and log it under the "External Reports" tab in ClaimSphere.

## **5\. Next Steps and Triage**

* **Towing:** If the vehicle is non-drivable, dispatch roadside assistance via our partner network, Agero.  
* **Rental Car:** If the policy includes Rental Reimbursement (Coverage Code: RR-01), refer them to Enterprise Rent-A-Car and provide their claim number as the direct billing reference.  
* **Adjuster Assignment:** Read verbatim: "I have successfully filed your claim. Your claim number is \[Claim Number\]. A dedicated National Sentinel claims adjuster will contact you within 24 hours to discuss the repair process."

## **6\. Closing the Call**

* Summarize actions taken.  
* Ask: "Is there anything else I can assist you with today?"  
* Close with: "Thank you for trusting National Sentinel Insurance. Please stay safe."
