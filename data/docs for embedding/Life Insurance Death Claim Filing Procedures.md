# Life Insurance Death Claim — Filing Procedures

**Department:** Claims — Life Insurance  
**Document ID:** KB-LIFE-001  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Classification:** Internal Use — Agent Reference

---

## Overview

This document outlines the standard procedure for processing life insurance death claims at National Sentinel Insurance. Agents must handle these calls with exceptional sensitivity and professionalism.

## Procedure

1. **Express condolences** to the caller sincerely and professionally. Use natural language — do not read from a script.
2. **Locate the policy** using the policy number or the registered phone number on file.
3. **Verify the caller's identity** — confirm the caller's full legal name against the beneficiary records on file. If the caller is not a listed beneficiary, they may still file the claim, but payout will be directed to the named beneficiaries.
4. **Record the following details about the insured's passing:**
   - Full legal name of the deceased
   - Date of death
   - Location of death (city, state)
   - Cause of death (handle with sensitivity — do not press for details beyond what is voluntarily offered)

## Required Documents

All of the following must be submitted before the claim can be processed:

| Document | Details |
|----------|---------|
| **Death Certificate** | Certified original or certified copy issued by the state/county |
| **Beneficiary Claim Form** | Will be mailed or emailed to the caller after the claim is initiated |
| **Government-Issued Photo ID** | Copy of the claimant's driver's license, passport, or state ID |
| **Supplemental Forms** | For accidental death claims: accident report, police report, or autopsy report |

## Payout Information

- **Processing timeline:** Claims are typically processed within **30–60 days** after all documents are received and verified.
- **Payout options available:**
  - Lump sum payment
  - Installment payments (monthly/quarterly)
  - Annuity option (lifetime income stream)
- The beneficiary selects their preferred payout method on the claim form.

## Contestability Period

- If the death occurred within the **2-year contestability period** from policy inception, the claim is subject to additional review by the Underwriting department.
- During this period, the insurer has the right to investigate material misrepresentations on the original application.
- If the policy is past the contestability period, it cannot be contested except in cases of proven fraud.

## Suicide Clause

- Most policies contain a **suicide exclusion** for the first 2 years. If the cause of death is suicide and the policy is within this period, the insurer will refund premiums paid but will not pay the death benefit.
- Handle with extreme sensitivity. Do NOT volunteer information about the suicide clause unless directly asked by the claimant.

## Compliance Notes

- All medical and personal information is protected under **HIPAA**.
- Do NOT disclose policy benefit amounts, beneficiary information, or medical details to unauthorized persons.
- Verify caller authorization before proceeding with any information sharing.
