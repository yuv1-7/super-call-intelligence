# Insurance Compliance Rules — Agent Quick Reference

**Department:** Compliance & Legal  
**Document ID:** COMP-ALL-001  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Classification:** Internal Use — MANDATORY Agent Reference

---

## Overview

This document contains all compliance rules that agents must follow during FNOL and claims calls. Violations of critical rules may result in disciplinary action, claim denial, or legal liability.

---

## General Compliance (All Call Types)

### Call Recording Disclosure
**Severity:** Medium  
Ensure the caller has been informed that this call may be recorded for quality and training purposes. This disclosure is legally required in most jurisdictions. The recording notice should be given at the beginning of every call.

### Anti-Fraud Protocol
**Severity:** Medium  
If fraud is suspected, do NOT accuse the caller. Document observations factually and flag the claim for Special Investigations Unit (SIU) review. Any person who knowingly files a false insurance claim is subject to criminal prosecution.

## Auto Insurance Compliance

### State Reporting Deadline
**Severity:** High  
Most states require accident reports to be filed within 10 days. Remind the policyholder to file a police report as soon as possible if not already done. Failure to report may jeopardize the claim.

### Do Not Admit Fault Advisory
**Severity:** CRITICAL  
Advise the policyholder NEVER to admit fault at the scene or to any third party. Fault determination is made by the insurance company based on evidence and investigation. Admitting fault may void coverage.

### Salvage Title Disclosure
**Severity:** Medium  
If the vehicle is declared a total loss, inform the policyholder that the title will be converted to a salvage title. This must be disclosed if the vehicle is ever resold. Failure to disclose is a violation in most states.

### Uninsured Motorist Notification
**Severity:** High  
If the other driver is uninsured or it's a hit-and-run, check if the policyholder has Uninsured/Underinsured Motorist (UM/UIM) coverage. They have the right to file under their own UM/UIM policy.

## Life Insurance Compliance

### HIPAA Privacy Notice
**Severity:** CRITICAL  
All medical and personal information discussed on this call is protected under HIPAA. Do NOT disclose policy details, medical information, or beneficiary information to unauthorized parties. Verify caller identity before proceeding.

### Contestability Period Warning
**Severity:** High  
Check if the policy is within the 2-year contestability period. If so, the insurer has the right to investigate and potentially deny the claim for material misrepresentation on the application. Flag the claim for underwriting review.

### Beneficiary Dispute Protocol
**Severity:** High  
If there is a beneficiary dispute or if the caller mentions a divorce or separated spouse, the claim must be placed on HOLD. Escalate immediately to the Legal department. Do NOT discuss payout amounts or timelines.

### Suicide Clause Advisory
**Severity:** CRITICAL  
Most life insurance policies contain a suicide exclusion clause for the first 2 years. Handle this topic with extreme sensitivity. Do NOT volunteer information about the suicide clause unless directly asked. Document the cause of death as reported and flag for Special Claims review.

## Medical Insurance Compliance

### Notification Timing Requirement
**Severity:** High  
The insurer must be notified within 24 hours of planned hospitalization and within 48 hours for emergency admissions. Remind the caller to ensure timely notification. Late notification may result in claim processing delays.

### Pre-Authorization Mandatory for In-Network
**Severity:** CRITICAL  
For in-network hospitalization claims, pre-authorization from the claims department is MANDATORY before treatment begins (except in emergencies). Without pre-authorization, the claim will be converted to out-of-network reimbursement. Ensure the hospital's insurance desk submits the pre-authorization request.

### HIPAA Medical Information Privacy
**Severity:** CRITICAL  
All medical and personal health information discussed on this call is protected under HIPAA. Do NOT disclose diagnosis, treatment details, or claim information to unauthorized parties. Verify that the caller is the policyholder or an authorized representative before sharing any medical claim details.

### Pre-Existing Condition Disclosure
**Severity:** High  
If the claim involves a pre-existing condition, check the waiting period status. If the 4-year waiting period has NOT expired, the claim may be excluded. Communicate waiting period status clearly and sensitively. Non-disclosure of pre-existing conditions at policy inception may result in claim rejection — flag for underwriting review if suspected.

## Severity Definitions

| Level | Definition | Action |
|-------|-----------|--------|
| **CRITICAL** | Violation may cause immediate legal liability, regulatory action, or claim denial | Agent must comply immediately; violations are reported to management |
| **High** | Non-compliance may impact claim validity or policyholder rights | Agent must address during the call; documented in QA review |
| **Medium** | Best practice that supports quality standards and regulatory compliance | Addressed during coaching; included in QA scoring |
