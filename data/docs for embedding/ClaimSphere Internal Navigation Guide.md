# **ClaimSphere: Internal Navigation & UI Guide**

**Document ID:** SYS-UI-NS-005  
**Company:** National Sentinel Insurance  
**System Role:** Software Navigation Manual for AI and Agents

## **1\. The Dashboard (Policy Verification)**

When an agent accesses a policy via phone number or NS- policy number, they land on the **Dashboard**.

* **Top Ribbon:** Displays the Named Insured, Policy Status (Active/Cancelled), and the 17-character VIN.  
* **Coverage Widget:** Located on the right side of the Dashboard, this widget displays BI limits, PD limits, Deductibles, and Add-on Codes (e.g., RR-01).

## **2\. The FNOL Intake Form**

To start a new claim, click the blue **"Initiate FNOL"** button on the Dashboard. This opens the Intake Form, which is divided into specific tabs:

* **Tab 1: Incident Basics:** Captures Date/Time, Location, and Weather.  
* **Tab 2: Description & Damage:** Captures the customer's statement, drivability, and point of impact. Includes the Agero dispatch button for towing.  
* **Tab 3: Third Party:** Captures other driver and vehicle information.

## **3\. Specialized Tabs**

* **Medical Intake Tab:** This is a highly secured, restricted-access tab. All injury data (Protected Health Information / PHI) MUST be entered here. Do not type injury details into the general "Description & Damage" text box to avoid HIPAA violations.  
* **External Reports Tab:** Used specifically to log Police Report numbers, Fire Department incident numbers, and responding precinct information.

## **4\. Location Ping Tool**

If a customer is stranded and doesn't know their location (common during RA-01 or towing scenarios), agents can use the **Location Ping Tool**.

* **Location:** Top navigation bar \-\> "Tools" \-\> "Send Location Ping".  
* **Function:** Sends an SMS text message to the customer's phone. When the customer clicks the link, their GPS coordinates are automatically imported into the ClaimSphere Intake Form.
