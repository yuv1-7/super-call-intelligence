# Vehicle Vandalism Claims — Standard Operating Procedure

**Department:** Claims — Auto Insurance  
**Document ID:** KB-CAR-004  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Classification:** Internal Use — Agent Reference

---

## Overview

This document outlines the standard procedure for handling vehicle vandalism claims under National Sentinel Insurance auto policies.

## Eligibility

- Vandalism falls under **Comprehensive coverage**. The policyholder must hold a Comprehensive policy for the claim to be valid.
- Third-Party Only liability policies do **not** cover vandalism damage.

## Procedure

1. Confirm that the policyholder has active Comprehensive coverage.
2. A **police report is required** for all vandalism claims — collect the report number if available.
3. Document the type of damage:
   - Keyed paint
   - Broken windows
   - Slashed tires
   - Graffiti
   - Body panel dents from intentional damage
4. **Photographs** of the damage are required (minimum 4 angles): front, rear, and both sides showing the damage.
5. The standard **Comprehensive deductible** applies (typically $250 or $500 depending on the policy).
6. An adjuster visit will be scheduled, or the caller may visit an approved repair facility for an estimate.
7. Filing a vandalism claim generally does **not** affect the policyholder's premium.

## Required Documents

- Police report (report number required)
- Photographs of damage (minimum 4 angles)
- Repair estimate from approved facility (optional at intake — adjuster will handle)

## Processing Timeline

- Adjuster assignment: within 24–48 hours of claim filing
- Repair authorization: within 3–5 business days of adjuster inspection
- Payment: issued within 10 business days of repair authorization

## Notes

- If the vehicle was unattended in a public area, advise the policyholder to check for any surveillance camera footage nearby and preserve it if possible.
- If the vandalism appears targeted or related to a personal dispute, note this in the claim file for SIU awareness.
