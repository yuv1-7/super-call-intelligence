# **Day-Care & Outpatient (OPD) Claim Procedures**

**Document ID:** SOP-MED-NS-003  
**Last Updated:** March 1, 2026  
**Department:** Claims Customer Service  
**Applies To:** All Medical Claims Personnel  
**System Role:** National Sentinel Insurance Procedure Reference

## **1\. Purpose**

This document outlines the procedures for processing day-care and outpatient (OPD) claims under National Sentinel Medical Insurance policies.

## **2\. Day-Care Procedures**

### **2.1 Definition**

Day-care procedures are medical treatments or surgeries that require less than 24 hours of hospitalization. The patient is admitted and discharged on the same day.

### **2.2 Commonly Covered Day-Care Procedures**

* Cataract surgery with lens implant  
* Dialysis (hemodialysis, peritoneal dialysis)  
* Chemotherapy and radiation therapy  
* Lithotripsy (kidney/gallbladder stone treatment)  
* Tonsillectomy and adenoidectomy  
* Dental surgery under general anesthesia  
* Arthroscopy (knee, shoulder)  
* Endoscopy and colonoscopy  
* Sinus surgery  
* Laser eye surgery (LASIK, PRK)

### **2.3 Coverage Requirements**

* The policyholder must have the **'Day-Care Procedures' add-on** or a comprehensive plan that includes day-care benefits.  
* If the add-on is not present, the claim is **not covered** under the day-care benefit.  
* Day-care claims follow the same in-network / out-of-network process as regular hospitalization claims.  
* Pre-authorization is **required** for in-network day-care procedures.

### **2.4 Claim Process**

1. Verify the policyholder has day-care coverage.  
2. Determine if the facility is in-network or out-of-network.  
3. For in-network: The hospital's insurance desk submits a pre-authorization request. Approval is typically within 2 hours.  
4. For out-of-network: The policyholder pays upfront and submits bills for reimbursement within 30 days.  
5. The copay and deductible apply as per the policy terms.

## **3\. Outpatient (OPD) Claims**

### **3.1 Definition**

OPD benefits cover medical expenses incurred without hospitalization: doctor consultations, diagnostic tests, pharmacy prescriptions, physiotherapy sessions, and minor procedures.

### **3.2 Coverage Requirements**

* OPD is covered **only** if the policyholder has the **'OPD Cover' add-on**.  
* Without the add-on, OPD expenses are not reimbursable.

### **3.3 Claim Process**

1. The policyholder pays all OPD expenses upfront.  
2. Required documents for reimbursement:  
   * Doctor's prescription or referral  
   * Diagnostic test reports  
   * Pharmacy bills with receipts  
   * Consultation fee receipts  
3. Documents must be submitted within 30 days of the visit.  
4. OPD claims are typically processed within **15 business days** of document submission.

### **3.4 Annual OPD Limits**

* Each policy has an annual OPD reimbursement limit, typically between **$3,000 and $5,000** depending on the plan tier.  
* Once the annual limit is exhausted, no further OPD reimbursements are available until the next policy year.  
* Agents should inform the policyholder of their remaining OPD balance when processing a claim.

## **4\. Agent Guidelines**

* Always verify day-care or OPD coverage **before** advising the policyholder on the claim process.  
* If the policyholder does not have the required add-on, inform them clearly and suggest they consider adding it at their next renewal.  
* For recurring treatments (e.g., dialysis, chemotherapy), advise the policyholder to get pre-authorization for the full treatment course to avoid repeated paperwork.
