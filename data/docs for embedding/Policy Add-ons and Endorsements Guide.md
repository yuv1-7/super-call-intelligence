**System Role:** Source of Truth for Policy Add-ons and Limits

## **RR-01: Rental Reimbursement Coverage**

This add-on provides the insured with a temporary rental vehicle while their primary vehicle is in the shop for covered repairs, or if it is deemed a total loss.

* **Daily Limit:** $40 per day.  
* **Maximum Limit:** $1,200 per occurrence (Up to 30 days).  
* **Vendor Partner:** Enterprise Rent-A-Car.  
* **Procedure:** Agents must provide the customer's National Sentinel Claim Number to Enterprise to establish a direct-bill account. Customers are responsible for any daily overages above $40 (e.g., if they choose to upgrade to a luxury SUV), as well as fuel costs and optional rental insurance.

## **RA-01: Roadside Assistance Coverage**

Provides emergency dispatch services for non-drivable vehicles due to mechanical breakdown, flat tire, dead battery, lockout, or running out of fuel.

* **Maximum Limit:** $150 per incident.  
* **Vendor Partner:** Agero.  
* **Procedure:** Described in full in KB-ESP-NS-002. Dispatched directly through the ClaimSphere integration. Towing related to an accident/collision is handled under standard Collision coverage, NOT RA-01.

## **RS-02: Rideshare / TNC Endorsement**

Standard personal auto policies exclude coverage when a vehicle is being used for a Transportation Network Company (TNC) like Uber or Lyft. The RS-02 endorsement bridges the "coverage gap".

* **Period 1 (App On, No Passenger):** RS-02 provides primary liability and physical damage coverage while the driver is logged into the rideshare app but has not yet accepted a ride.  
* **Period 2 & 3 (En Route & Passenger in Car):** RS-02 coverage drops to secondary. The TNC's commercial insurance policy (e.g., Uber's insurance) becomes the primary coverage.  
* **FNOL Handling:** If a customer with RS-02 calls to report an accident, the agent MUST ask: "Were you logged into a rideshare app at the time of the accident? Did you have a passenger in the vehicle?" Document this exactly in the ClaimSphere Intake Form.
