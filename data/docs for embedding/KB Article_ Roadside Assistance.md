# **Knowledge Base: Glass and Windshield Claims**

**Article ID:** KB-GLS-NS-104  
**Topic:** Comprehensive Auto Claims \- Glass Repair and Replacement  
**Company:** National Sentinel Insurance

## **Overview**

Glass claims (windshields, side windows, rear windows, and sunroofs) are the most frequent type of claim filed at National Sentinel. They fall strictly under the **Comprehensive Coverage** section of the policy.

## **Repair vs. Replacement Guidelines**

National Sentinel Insurance partners exclusively with Safelite AutoGlass to provide mobile repair services.  
**When to Authorize a REPAIR (Resin Injection):**

* The chip or crack is smaller than a standard dollar bill (approx. 6 inches).  
* There are three (3) or fewer chips on the windshield.  
* The damage is NOT in the driver's direct line of sight.  
* *Deductible:* In most states, if a National Sentinel customer opts for a repair instead of a replacement, the comprehensive deductible is **waived** ($0 out of pocket for the customer).

**When to Authorize a REPLACEMENT:**

* The crack is larger than a dollar bill.  
* The damage is in the driver's direct line of sight (could impair vision).  
* The damage is to a tempered glass panel (side windows, rear windows, sunroofs). Tempered glass shatters and cannot be repaired; it must be replaced.  
* *Deductible:* If a replacement is required, the customer's standard Comprehensive Deductible applies (e.g., $250 or $500), unless they reside in a "Zero Deductible Glass State".

## **Zero Deductible Glass States**

By state law, policyholders in the following states do not have to pay a deductible for glass replacement if they carry Comprehensive coverage:

* Florida (Windshield only)  
* Kentucky (All glass)  
* South Carolina (All glass)

## **ADAS Calibration (Advanced Driver Assistance Systems)**

Modern vehicles are equipped with cameras and sensors mounted to the windshield (e.g., lane departure warnings, automatic braking). If a windshield is *replaced*, these systems must be recalibrated.

* **Agent Action:** Inform the customer that recalibration may be required. Safelite technicians can perform this during the replacement appointment. Recalibration costs are covered under the National Sentinel glass claim without additional deductibles.
