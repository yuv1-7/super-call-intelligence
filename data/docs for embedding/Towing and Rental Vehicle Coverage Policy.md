# Towing & Rental Vehicle Coverage Policy

**Department:** Claims — Auto Insurance  
**Document ID:** KB-CAR-003  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Classification:** Internal Use — Agent Reference

---

## Overview

This document details the towing, roadside assistance, and rental vehicle reimbursement coverage available under National Sentinel Insurance auto policies.

## Towing & Roadside Assistance

### Eligibility
- Towing and Roadside Assistance is included with:
  - **Comprehensive** policies (included by default)
  - **Roadside Assistance add-on** (available for all policy tiers)
- Third-Party Only liability policies do **not** include towing or roadside coverage.

### Coverage Limits
- Covered towing is authorized up to **$500 per incident**.
- Roadside assistance includes: flat tire change, battery jump-start, lockout service, and fuel delivery (up to 2 gallons).
- Towing must be to the **nearest approved repair facility** or a facility of the policyholder's choice within the covered radius.

### Uncovered Policyholders
- For policyholders without towing coverage, towing and roadside services can still be **arranged through our partner network** but are billed directly to the customer as out-of-pocket expenses.

## Rental Vehicle Reimbursement

### Eligibility
- Rental vehicle reimbursement is available **only** with the **Rental Reimbursement add-on**.
- This add-on is available for Comprehensive and Collision policy tiers.

### Coverage Limits
- Maximum: **$75/day for up to 30 days** ($2,250 total per claim).
- Coverage begins from the day the vehicle is dropped off at the repair facility and ends when the vehicle is returned or the maximum is reached.
- The policyholder must use an **approved rental partner** to receive direct billing. Out-of-network rentals require upfront payment and reimbursement submission.

### Claims Without Rental Add-On
- If the policyholder does not have Rental Reimbursement, inform them that rental costs will be their responsibility.
- If the accident was **not the policyholder's fault**, advise them to pursue rental reimbursement through the at-fault party's insurance.

## Agent Checklist

1. Check policy for Comprehensive coverage or Roadside add-on before confirming towing eligibility.
2. Check for Rental Reimbursement add-on before confirming rental coverage.
3. If covered, provide the towing dispatch number or nearest approved facility.
4. If uncovered, offer to arrange service at the policyholder's expense.
5. Document towing and rental requests in the claim notes.
