# Fraud Indicators — Red Flags Reference Guide

**Department:** Claims — All Lines  
**Document ID:** KB-GEN-001  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2025  
**Classification:** Internal Use — Agent Reference — CONFIDENTIAL

---

## Overview

This reference guide helps agents identify potential fraud indicators during FNOL and claims calls. These indicators alone do not confirm fraud — they flag situations for further investigation by the Special Investigations Unit (SIU).

## Common Red Flags

### Timing-Based Indicators
- Claim filed **immediately after policy inception** or a recent coverage increase
- **Comprehensive coverage was recently added** before a theft, fire, or vandalism claim
- Policy was recently reinstated after a lapse, followed by a claim

### Behavioral Indicators
- Caller demonstrates **unusual familiarity** with the claims process, insurance terminology, or settlement procedures
- Description of events sounds **inconsistent, rehearsed, or overly detailed**
- Caller is **pressuring for an expedited settlement** or quick payout
- Caller becomes **evasive or hostile** when asked routine verification questions
- Caller provides **conflicting information** at different points in the call

### Circumstantial Indicators
- Incident occurred in a **remote location with no witnesses**
- Vehicle was **recently listed for sale** prior to a theft or total loss claim
- **Multiple prior claims** filed within a short period (check claim history)
- Claimed injuries are **disproportionate** to the described accident severity
- Vehicle had **pre-existing damage** that the caller attributes to the current incident
- Loss involves **expensive items** without receipts or proof of ownership

### Medical Claim Red Flags
- Hospitalization at a facility **far from the policyholder's home** without clear reason
- Diagnosis does **not match** the treatment received or the length of hospitalization
- Repeated claims for the **same condition** across short time intervals
- Bills include charges for procedures that are **not standard** for the stated diagnosis

## Agent Protocol

1. **Do NOT confront or accuse** the caller of fraud under any circumstances.
2. **Document observations** factually and neutrally in the claim notes.
3. **Flag the claim** for SIU review using the claim flagging system.
4. **Continue the call professionally** — process the FNOL normally while documenting red flags.
5. **Do NOT deny the claim** — only SIU has the authority to make fraud determinations.

## Legal Notice

Any person who knowingly files a false or fraudulent insurance claim is subject to criminal prosecution and civil penalties under applicable state and federal law.
