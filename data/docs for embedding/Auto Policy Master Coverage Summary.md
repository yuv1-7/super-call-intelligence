# **Comprehensive Auto Insurance Policy Summary & Definitions**

**Document ID:** POL-AUTO-NS-2026  
**Effective Date:** January 1, 2026  
**Company:** National Sentinel Insurance  
**System Role:** Source of Truth for Policy Limits and Definitions

## **Part A: Liability Coverage**

### **Bodily Injury Liability (BI)**

Bodily Injury Liability coverage protects the National Sentinel insured if they are legally responsible for an automobile accident that causes physical injury or death to another person.

* **Coverage Includes:** Medical expenses, lost wages, pain and suffering, and legal defense costs up to the policy limit.  
* **Structure:** Structured as split limits (e.g., $100,000 per person / $300,000 per accident).  
* **Exclusions:** Does not cover injuries to the insured or the insured's own family members residing in the same household. Does not cover intentional acts of violence using the vehicle.

### **Property Damage Liability (PD)**

Property Damage Liability coverage pays for damages the insured legally causes to another person's property as a result of an automobile accident.

* **Coverage Includes:** Repair or replacement of third-party vehicles, structural damage (fences, storefronts, utility poles), and legal defense costs.  
* **Exclusions:** Does not cover damage to the insured's own vehicle or property owned by the insured.

## **Part B: Vehicle Coverages (Physical Damage)**

### **Collision Coverage**

Collision coverage pays for damage to the insured vehicle resulting from a collision with another vehicle or object (e.g., a tree, guardrail, or pothole), or if the vehicle rolls over.

* **Payout Basis:** Actual Cash Value (ACV) minus the deductible. ACV is defined as the replacement cost of the vehicle minus depreciation.  
* **Deductible:** Applies per occurrence. Standard National Sentinel deductibles are $250, $500, or $1,000.

### **Comprehensive Coverage (Other Than Collision)**

Comprehensive coverage pays for damage to the insured vehicle caused by events outside of the insured's control, excluding collisions.

* **Covered Perils Include:** Theft, vandalism, fire, natural disasters (hail, floods, hurricanes, tornadoes), falling objects (tree branches), and striking an animal (e.g., a deer).  
* **Glass Damage:** Windshield and glass repair/replacement fall under Comprehensive coverage.

## **Part C: Uninsured / Underinsured Motorist Coverage (UM/UIM)**

This coverage protects the insured and their passengers if they are injured by a driver who does not carry liability insurance (Uninsured) or whose liability limits are too low to cover the full extent of the damages (Underinsured).

* **UMBI (Bodily Injury):** Covers medical bills, lost wages, and pain and suffering.  
* **UMPD (Property Damage):** Covers damage to the insured vehicle. Note: UMPD is not available in all states. In states where it is unavailable, Collision coverage must be used.  
* **Hit-and-Run:** Hit-and-run accidents are covered under UM/UIM, provided the incident is reported to the police within 24 hours.

## **Part E: General Exclusions**

The following scenarios are explicitly excluded from coverage under any National Sentinel Auto Policy:

1. **Ridesharing / Livery:** Using the vehicle to carry passengers for a fee (e.g., Uber, Lyft) or delivering goods (e.g., DoorDash, Amazon Flex) without the specific National Sentinel RS-02 rideshare endorsement.  
2. **Racing:** Any damage or injury occurring while the vehicle is participating in a pre-arranged or organized racing or speed contest.  
3. **Intentional Acts:** Any injury or damage intentionally caused by the insured.
